## 6. SYMPTOM-TO-DEPARTMENT TRIAGE GUIDE

**Use this section to guide the AI Symptom Checker logic.**

### 6.1 Route to General Medicine
- Fever, headache, body aches, chills (possible malaria/typhoid)
- Cough, cold, sore throat without severe ear pain or hearing loss
- Stomach pain, diarrhea, vomiting
- Rash, skin infections, minor burns
- High blood pressure check, diabetes follow-up
- Routine vaccination, wellness exam

**Urgency Rules:**
- Fever > 39°C with confusion → **emergency** (refer to nearest ER)
- Severe dehydration from vomiting/diarrhea → **urgent**
- Mild cold symptoms > 3 days → **routine**

### 6.2 Route to ENT
- Ear pain, discharge, hearing loss, ringing in ears
- Nasal blockage > 2 weeks, recurrent nosebleeds
- Persistent sore throat, difficulty swallowing, hoarseness > 3 weeks
- Snoring with daytime sleepiness (possible sleep apnea)
- Foreign body in ear or nose

**Urgency Rules:**
- Sudden complete hearing loss in one ear → **urgent** (same day)
- Nosebleed not stopping after 20 minutes of pressure → **emergency**
- Foreign body in airway causing breathing difficulty → **emergency**

### 6.3 Route to Maternity
- Pregnancy confirmation or positive home test
- Routine antenatal care
- Reduced fetal movements (after 24 weeks)
- Vaginal bleeding during pregnancy
- Post-delivery check-up (6 weeks)
- Family planning after delivery

**Urgency Rules:**
- Heavy vaginal bleeding during pregnancy → **emergency**
- Severe abdominal pain with dizziness in pregnancy → **emergency**
- Water breaking (ruptured membranes) → **urgent** (proceed to maternity unit immediately)

### 6.4 General Pre-Visit Instructions by Urgency
| Urgency | Action |
|---|---|
| Routine | Book next available slot; no special preparation unless told. |
| Urgent | Same-day appointment if available; patient should arrive within 2 hours. |
| Emergency | Advise patient to proceed immediately to nearest emergency department or call 112. Do not wait for appointment. |

## 8. PATIENT EDUCATION CONTENT LIBRARY

### 8.1 Pre-Procedure Guides

**General Blood Test (FBC, LFT, RFT, Lipid Profile):**
- Fast for 8–12 hours before the test (water is allowed).
- Take morning medications only with water unless told otherwise.
- Avoid alcohol for 24 hours before the test.
- Wear loose clothing to allow easy access to the arm.

**Ultrasound (Abdominal):**
- Fast for 6–8 hours before the scan.
- Drink water and arrive with a full bladder for pelvic scans.
- Wear comfortable, two-piece clothing.

**ENT Endoscopy (Nasal):**
- Avoid eating or drinking 2 hours before the procedure.
- Avoid nasal sprays or decongestants 6 hours prior unless prescribed.
- The procedure takes 5–10 minutes and may cause mild discomfort or tearing.

**Hearing Test (Audiometry):**
- Avoid loud noise exposure for 24 hours before the test.
- Remove earwax blockages beforehand if advised.
- The test is painless and takes about 20 minutes in a soundproof booth.

### 8.2 Post-Care Instructions

**After Malaria Treatment:**
- Complete the full course of antimalarial medication even if you feel better.
- Drink plenty of fluids to stay hydrated.
- Return if fever persists after 48 hours of treatment.

**After Ear Wax Removal:**
- Keep the ear dry for 24 hours (no swimming or water entry).
- Avoid inserting cotton buds or objects into the ear.
- Mild discomfort is normal; severe pain or discharge requires a follow-up.

**After Normal Vaginal Delivery:**
- Rest as much as possible for the first 6 weeks.
- Keep the perineal area clean and dry; change pads frequently.
- Attend the 6-week post-natal check-up.
- Seek immediate care for heavy bleeding, fever, or severe abdominal pain.

### 8.3 Dietary Notes by Condition
| Condition | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Hypertension | Reduce salt intake; avoid processed foods; increase fruits and vegetables. |
| Diabetes | Avoid sugary drinks and refined carbs; eat small, frequent meals; monitor blood sugar. |
| Pregnancy (ANC) | Take iron and folic acid supplements; eat protein-rich foods; avoid raw fish and unpasteurized dairy. |
| Post-ENT Surgery | Soft foods for 24–48 hours; avoid very hot drinks and spicy foods. |
| High Cholesterol | Reduce fried foods and red meat; increase fibre (oats, beans); use vegetable oils sparingly. |

## 9. QUEUE & WAIT TIME SYSTEM RULES

### 9.1 How Check-In Works
1. Patient arrives and checks in at reception or via online check-in.
2. Patient receives a queue number and an estimated wait time.
3. Real-time updates are pushed to the patient's phone every 30 seconds via WebSocket.
4. When the doctor is ready, the patient is called by name/number.

### 9.2 Wait Time Estimation Logic
- Base estimate = (number of patients ahead × average consultation duration for that department) − elapsed time.
- General Medicine: 25 min avg. | ENT: 30 min avg. | Maternity: 35 min avg.
- If a doctor is delayed (e.g., emergency case), a buffer message is sent: *"Your doctor is attending to an urgent case. Your wait time has been extended by approximately X minutes."*

### 9.3 Patient Communication Templates
- **Check-In Confirmation:** *"You are checked in. Your position in queue: [X]. Estimated wait: [Y] minutes. We will notify you when it's your turn."*
- **Queue Update:** *"You are now [X] in queue. Estimated wait: [Y] minutes."*
- **Ready Notification:** *"The doctor is ready to see you. Please proceed to [Room/Consultation Suite]."*

## 10. TESTIMONIALS & FEEDBACK SYSTEM

### 10.1 Feedback Collection Process
- 2 hours after the visit, an SMS/WhatsApp survey is sent automatically.
- Survey asks: (1) Overall rating (1–5 stars), (2) What went well?, (3) What could improve?, (4) Would you recommend us?
- Patients who rate 4–5 stars are invited to provide a public testimonial.
- Patients who rate 1–3 stars trigger an internal alert for service recovery (manager follow-up within 24 hours).

### 10.2 Consent & Publishing Rules
- Testimonials are never published without explicit patient consent.
- Consent is collected via reply to the feedback SMS: *"Reply YES to allow us to share your feedback on our website."*
- Published testimonials show first name and last initial only (e.g., "Ama K.") to protect privacy.
- AI distills raw feedback into polished but authentic-sounding testimonials; the core sentiment and facts are preserved.

### 10.3 Sample Testimonial Style Guide
- Tone: Warm, grateful, authentic.
- Length: 1–3 sentences.
- Focus: Specific staff member, short wait time, clean facility, or successful outcome.
- Example: *"The staff at Biney Medical Centre were so kind during my antenatal visits. Dr. Ama took time to answer all my questions, and the online booking made everything easy."* — Grace O.

## 12. PROHIBITED & RESTRICTED CONTENT

- **Never diagnose definitively.** The AI can suggest likely departments and urgency levels but must always include: *"This is not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a doctor for proper evaluation."*
- **Never prescribe medication.** The AI can mention common over-the-counter remedies (e.g., paracetamol for mild fever) but must advise seeing a clinician for prescriptions.
- **Never share other patients' data.**
- **Never provide false certainty.** Use confidence scores and encourage professional consultation.
- **Never quote exact prices** without a disclaimer that prices are subject to change.
